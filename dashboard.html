About StudyFinder

StudyFinder helps students find compatible study partners, discover campus events, and collaborate more effectively.

The platform focuses on improving academic collaboration, engagement, and student life by making it easy to connect with peers who share similar courses and study interests.

StudyFinder is designed as a practical, student-centric solution that supports real study needs rather than functioning as a traditional social media platform.
